# 贡献者行为准则 · Code of Conduct

**简体中文** · [English](#english)

本准则参考 [Contributor Covenant v2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/)
编写，并结合「OBS 排障助手」项目的实际情况做了落地化说明。
中英两个版本**都是正式版本**；两者若有出入，以**中文版**为准。

> **语言**：中文或英文都可以，**也可以用中英双语**。
> 我们不会因为语言不地道而降低对一条反馈的重视。

## 我们的承诺

作为本项目的参与者、贡献者与维护者，我们承诺：
让每一个人参与本项目的体验都不受骚扰——无论其年龄、体型、可见或不可见的身体特征、
种族、民族、性别认同与表达、经验水平、学历、社会经济状况、国籍、外貌、宗教或性取向。

我们承诺以开放、友善、包容的方式行事，共同营造一个健康、专业、对新手友好的协作环境。

## 期望行为

我们期望所有参与者：

- **对他人保持尊重与体谅**，注意不同经验水平的人对同一个说法的理解可能不同。
- **给出可操作的反馈**：指出问题时说明「环境 / 步骤 / 现象 / 期望」，而不是只下结论。
- **优雅地接受建设性批评**，把它当作改进产品的输入，而不是对个人的评价。
- **聚焦对社区最有利的事情**，而不是在争论中「赢」。
- **对其他社区成员表示同理心**，尤其是刚接触 OBS 或本工具的新用户。
- **遵守本仓库的技术约定**（见 [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md)），例如界面文案中英成对、
  新增纯逻辑带单元测试、提交信息使用既有风格。

## 不可接受的行为

以下行为在本项目中不可接受：

- 带有性暗示的言语、图像或不受欢迎的性关注与追求。
- 挑衅、侮辱、贬损性评论，以及针对个人或政治立场的人身攻击。
- 公开或私下的骚扰、跟踪、威胁。
- 未经明确许可，公开他人的私人信息（真实姓名、住址、邮箱、账号、聊天记录截图等）。
- 在反馈中提交他人的隐私内容，例如 **OBS 流密钥、账号密码、身份证件、未脱敏的完整日志与推流地址**。
- 其他在专业协作场景中明显不恰当的行为。
- 恶意行为：例如提交后门代码、故意破坏构建、滥用 Issue/PR 通道刷屏或投放广告。

## 适用范围

本准则适用于**所有项目空间**，包括但不限于：

- 本仓库的 Issue、Pull Request、Commit 评论与 Discussion；
- 与项目相关的邮件、私信等一对一沟通（当沟通内容涉及项目事务时）；
- 项目维护者在公开场合代表本项目时的言行。

同时适用于项目维护者与所有贡献者。当个人在项目之外的行为对项目社区造成实质影响时，
维护者同样有权依据本准则处理。

## 执行责任

项目维护者（[@YYRMMAYO](https://github.com/YYRMMAYO)）负责解释与执行本准则，并有权对
任何其认为不当、威胁、冒犯或有害的行为采取适当措施。

维护者应：

- 对举报内容**保密**，不向无关人员披露举报者身份与事件细节；
- 在处置时说明理由，并给出可申诉的通道；
- 对自身适用同一标准；
- 若被举报对象是维护者本人，举报者可要求在处置中回避。

## 处置梯度

处置措施由轻到重分级执行，具体采用哪一级取决于行为的性质、严重程度、持续时间与是否累犯：

| 级别 | 措施 | 适用情形（示例） |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | **提醒（Correction）** | 措辞不当、轻微跑题、无意的失礼。维护者私下或在帖内提醒，说明为什么不合适，必要时要求更正。 |
| 2 | **警告（Warning）** | 一次明显的冒犯行为或连续的不当言论。给出正式警告，并说明再次发生将升级处置；可要求在一段时间内不再与相关人员互动。 |
| 3 | **临时封禁（Temporary Ban）** | 严重违规，或收到警告后仍继续。在一段明确期限内禁止以任何方式参与项目（Issue / PR / 评论 / 邮件）。 |
| 4 | **永久封禁（Permanent Ban）** | 持续违规、骚扰个人、公开他人隐私、恶意破坏项目等。永久禁止参与项目。 |

对于**特别恶劣或紧急**的情况（如人身威胁、泄露他人隐私），维护者可跳过前置级别直接采取
临时或永久封禁，并在事后向社区说明。

## 举报方式

如果你遭遇或目睹了违反本准则的行为，请通过以下渠道联系维护者：

1. **邮件 <752139192@qq.com>（建议优先使用）** —— 涉及个人隐私、人身安全、
   或需要保密的举报，**请走这个渠道**；邮件标题以 `[CoC]` 开头便于识别。
2. **在本仓库提 Issue 并 @YYRMMAYO**（<https://github.com/YYRMMAYO/OBS_Helper/issues>）——
   仅适用于**不涉及个人隐私**的普遍性问题（例如希望补充某条社区规则）。

> **为什么不写「GitHub 私信」**：GitHub 并没有提供面向任意用户的私信功能，
> 以前写的「私信维护者」实际上无处可点。需要保密的沟通请一律走邮件。
> 邮件可以用中文或英文写。

请在举报中尽量说明：发生了什么、涉及谁、发生时间与地点、是否有截图或链接等证据。
所有举报都会被**及时、公正地**审阅与调查；维护者有义务保护举报者的隐私与安全。

# Code of Conduct

[简体中文](#贡献者行为准则code-of-conduct) · **English**

This document is based on the [Contributor Covenant v2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/)
and adapted to how this project actually operates. Both the Chinese and the English text are
official; if they ever disagree, the **Chinese version** prevails.

> **Language**: Chinese, English, or a mix of both. A report is never taken less seriously because
> of imperfect language.

## Our pledge

As participants, contributors and maintainers of this project, we pledge to make participation
harassment-free for everyone — regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability,
ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education,
socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and
orientation.

We pledge to act in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive and healthy
community, and to keep this project professional and friendly to newcomers.

## Expected behaviour

We expect all participants to:

- **be respectful and considerate** — different experience levels read the same words differently;
- **give actionable feedback**: describe environment / steps / symptom / expectation instead of only
  stating a conclusion;
- **accept constructive criticism gracefully**, treating it as input to improve the product rather
  than as a judgement of the person;
- **focus on what is best for the community**, rather than "winning" an argument;
- **show empathy towards other community members**, especially people new to OBS or to this tool;
- **follow this repository's technical conventions** (see [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md)) — for
  example bilingual UI text, unit tests for new pure logic, and the established commit style.

## Unacceptable behaviour

The following is unacceptable in this project:

- sexualized language or imagery, and unwelcome sexual attention or advances;
- trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks;
- public or private harassment, stalking, or threats;
- publishing others' private information (real name, address, email, account, chat screenshots)
  without explicit permission;
- submitting other people's private content in a report, such as **OBS stream keys, account
  passwords, identity documents, unredacted full logs and stream URLs**;
- other conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting;
- malicious behaviour: backdoor code, deliberately breaking the build, spamming issues or pull
  requests, or advertising.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to **all project spaces**, including but not limited to:

- issues, pull requests, commit comments and discussions in this repository;
- project-related one-to-one communication such as email or direct messages (when it concerns
  project business);
- the behaviour of maintainers when representing the project in public.

It applies to maintainers and contributors alike. Maintainers may also act on behaviour outside
these spaces when it has a material impact on the project community.

## Enforcement responsibilities

The maintainer ([@YYRMMAYO](https://github.com/YYRMMAYO)) is responsible for clarifying and enforcing
this Code of Conduct, and may take any action they deem appropriate in response to behaviour that is
inappropriate, threatening, offensive or harmful.

The maintainer will:

- keep reports **confidential**, not disclosing the reporter's identity or the incident details to
  uninvolved people;
- explain the reasoning behind any enforcement action, and offer a route to appeal;
- apply the same standard to themselves;
- step aside from handling a report when they are the subject of it, if the reporter asks.

## Enforcement ladder

Enforcement is graduated by severity; which level applies depends on the nature, severity, duration
and repetition of the behaviour:

| Level | Action | Applies to (examples) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | **Correction** | Inappropriate wording, mild derailment, unintended rudeness. A private or in-thread note explaining why it was inappropriate, with a correction requested where needed. |
| 2 | **Warning** | A clear offence or a series of inappropriate remarks. A formal warning stating that further occurrences will escalate; a period of no interaction with the people involved may be required. |
| 3 | **Temporary ban** | A serious violation, or continuing after a warning. Participation of any kind (issues / PRs / comments / email) is prohibited for a stated period. |
| 4 | **Permanent ban** | Sustained violations, harassment of an individual, publishing others' private information, or malicious damage to the project. Permanent exclusion. |

For **especially egregious or urgent** cases (threats to a person, leaking private information) the
maintainer may skip earlier levels and apply a temporary or permanent ban directly, explaining the
decision to the community afterwards.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness behaviour that violates this Code of Conduct, contact the maintainer:

1. **Email <752139192@qq.com> (preferred)** — for anything involving personal privacy, personal
   safety, or that needs to stay confidential, **please use this channel**; starting the subject
   line with `[CoC]` helps us identify it quickly.
2. **Open an issue in this repository and mention `@YYRMMAYO`**
   (<https://github.com/YYRMMAYO/OBS_Helper/issues>) — only for **general** questions that do not
   involve anyone's privacy (for example, asking us to add a community rule).

> **Why not "DM the maintainer on GitHub"**: GitHub does not offer direct messages between arbitrary
> users, so the older wording pointed at something that did not exist. Confidential communication
> goes through email. Emails may be written in Chinese or English.

Please describe in your report: what happened, who was involved, when and where it happened, and any
screenshots or links as evidence. All reports will be reviewed and investigated **promptly and
fairly**, and the privacy and safety of the reporter will be respected.
